METHOD
Each candidate is evaluated by its full 37-outcome payout distribution, not random simulated wheel outcomes.
Finite-horizon Bellman dynamic programming computes the selected policy for all bankrolls in $5 units and all remaining-spin counts.
A constraint-generation search adds improving portfolios and repeats until no two/three-position portfolio improves the value function within floating-point tolerance.
For a candidate total stake c, capital conservation gives E[next bankroll] = bankroll - c/37. Since continuation success probability <= next bankroll / 1200, candidates exceeding 37*(bankroll - 1200*incumbent_value) cannot improve the incumbent at that state. The screening program also accumulates nonnegative capital deficits to terminate losing candidates early.
Final maximum one-step improvement: ~1.3e-16 over pairs; ~8.4e-17 over triples; ~2.3e-16 over all 54,424 prior candidates. Finite 280-spin numerical bound is well below the reported precision.
No Monte Carlo error applies to the success probabilities. The sampling limitation concerns which larger portfolios were generated.

EXAMPLE
With $40 left, stake $30 on straight-up 0 and $10 on the 0-1-2 trio.
0 returns $1,200 total and reaches the target.
1 or 2 returns $120 total; the policy continues.

The equivalent straight-up 1 plus street 1-2-3 portfolio has the same distribution.
